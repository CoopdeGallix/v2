A store that belongs to Gallix.

Coop de Gallix is a solidarity co-operative. It exists to keep a general store open in the village, not to pay shareholders.

Our story

From then to now

In 2017, the only convenience store in Gallix closed and the village became a food desert. By 2018, residents had come together to do something about it.

After a public meeting that drew more than 100 people, the purchase of the lot on rue Thériault and years of volunteer work, the co-op opened its doors on January 16, 2025.

Key dates

  1. 2017The Gallix convenience store closes.
  2. 2018A provisional committee forms to answer the community’s need.
  3. 2022Public meeting with more than 100 people, then election of the first board of directors.
  4. 2025Coop de Gallix officially opens on January 16.

Mission

Take an active part in improving quality of life for the people of Gallix by ensuring access to essential goods and services.
Foster encounters and help Gallix shine by welcoming and promoting local products, activities and talent.
Build pride and a sense of belonging.

Vision

We aim to be a catalyst for the social and economic development of Gallix, with sustainable growth centred on the community’s present and future needs, and to help make Gallix a must-see stop for tourists and outdoor enthusiasts.

Our values: Welcome · Openness · Integrity · Mutual aid · Solidarity · Kindness

How a co-op works

1

Run by its members

One member, one vote. Major decisions are made at the general meeting.

2

Surpluses stay here

What the store earns is reinvested in the business and in the community.

3

Open to everyone

Member or not, resident or passing through: everyone can shop at the co-op.
