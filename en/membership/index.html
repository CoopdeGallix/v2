Become a member

Have a say in your store.

A one-time membership share of $100. In return, you get a say in the co-op's future.

What you get

A vote

One member, one vote at the annual general meeting, no matter how much you spend.

Member benefits

Discounts and offers reserved for members when you show your card.

A store that stays open

Every membership share helps keep the grocery, the hardware store and the pumps in the village.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be a member to shop at the co-op?
No. Everyone can shop at the co-op, member or not. Some benefits are reserved for members.
Is one membership per household enough?
Membership belongs to a person, not a household. Each individual who wants to become a user member fills out their own form and pays their own share.
Can children become members?
Under the bylaws, children cannot become members, but they can use their parents’ member card.
Do I have to renew every year?
No. Once you have paid your membership share, you remain a member for as long as you leave it in the co-op.
Do I have to live in Gallix?
Not at all. Anyone who wants to support the co-op can join, whether you are passing through, live nearby or live far away.
Where do I send an Interac e-Transfer?
Send it to adhesion@coopdegallix.ca.
